# TodoScope - Brand Interview

*A conversation with Alexander & Izzy, co-founders of Startr and Sage, on why a TODO scanner matters - and what it has to do with building a brand.*

---

**What is TodoScope?**

TodoScope makes the gap between what you intended and what you shipped visible. It finds every TODO, FIXME, and BUG hiding in your repos - before your clients or your AI assistant finds them for you.

It's also the awareness layer in Startr & Sage.is stack. Startr.Style is the look. Sage.is' the AI brain. TodoScope is what tells them both what's actually unfinished.

---

**Why does it exist?**

The closest an issue can live to its fix is right beside it in the code. TodoScope respects that with no context switching and no duplicate tracking. Your notes surface when you need them.

The best place to track an issue is next to the code that has it. We already were doing this with #TODOs. We just needed a way to see them all at once.

---

**Who is it for?**

Anyone building something real with a small team. If you're running lean, shipping continuously, and can't afford to lose track of things, this is for you.

That includes developers who use AI assistants and want those assistants working with live codebase context, not guessing. If you use Sage.is AI or Claude, TodoScope makes them meaningfully smarter about your work.

The startup, the agency, the small team who just needs to know what's left to do, these are people who TodoScope is for - people like us.

---

**What makes it different?**

Most tools tell you what's done. TodoScope tells you what *isn't.* That inversion is the whole product.

It's also built by the same people who built Sage.is and Startr.Style, with the same values: lightweight, open-source, no lock-in, and designed to talk to everything else in your stack.

---

**How does it connect to Sage.is AI?**

With Sage.is AI connected to TodoScope, you can ask your AI assistant "what's unfinished in this repo?" and get a real answer. It's live context, not guesswork.

Sage.is AI can see your #TODOs. And you can ask any of your agents to help you prioritize, assign, or close them. Your AI assistants are part of your team - and they will finally know what you haven't finished.

---

**Why open source?**

Because the tool is most useful when people trust it completely. Open source means you can see exactly what it does with your repos. No black box.

And because the people who need this most - small teams, solo founders, underdogs - shouldn't have to pay a subscription to know what's unfinished in their own code.

---

**What's the one-line pitch?**

See every #TODO across all your repos. The awareness layer for teams, including the AI agents on them, who need to know what's actually unfinished.

---

**Where does it go from here?**

The thing that closes the loop. Sage.is AI suggests a fix. You ship it. TodoScope confirms the #TODO is gone. You get: Intent → Action → Verification, all in one continuous thread.

A small tool today. The observability layer for the Startr ecosystem tomorrow. Every tool we
build talks to the others. TodoScope is how they all know what's left to do.

---

## Brand Voice Notes

- **Tone:** Honest founder voice, practitioner-grounded, clarity-first. Never buzzwordy.
- **AI agents are teammates**, not a feature - treat them as part of the team throughout.
- **The inversion is the hook:** most tools tell you what's done; TodoScope tells you what isn't.
- **Stay present tense:** the Sage.is AI integration is real now, not a roadmap item.
- **Keep it small and honest** - the tool earns trust by being open and unpretentious while
  pointing at something bigger: the fully aware, self-verifying Startr/Sage.is AI stack.
- **The flow principle:** issues live closest to the code that has them. No context switching,
  no duplicate systems. That's the founding insight - lead with it.
