# Startup & Branding Culture — Research for TodoScope

Reference document for the brand/startup voices informing TodoScope's positioning and copy.

## Startup Culture Thinkers

### Paul Graham — Y Combinator
Co-founder of YC. His essays on startups, ambition, and problem-solving shaped how a generation of founders thinks. Dense, opinionated, often contrarian. Key insight for us: build something people want, everything else is noise.

### Ben Horowitz — Culture Is What You Do
Co-founder of a16z. *The Hard Thing About Hard Things* and *What You Do Is Who You Are*. Draws on history (samurai, Genghis Khan, hip-hop) to argue culture is what you *do*, not what you say. Relevant: TodoScope's culture is showing — we open-sourced it, we self-host it, we dogfood it.

### Reid Hoffman — Masters of Scale
Co-founder of LinkedIn. Explores counterintuitive moves that make companies grow. Structured, thoughtful.

### Shane Parrish — Mental Models
The Knowledge Project / Farnam Street. Decision-making under uncertainty. Beloved by founders who want to think more clearly.

### The All-In Podcast Crew
Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, David Friedberg. Most listened-to voice in tech-startup discourse. Irreverent, provocative.

## Brand & Marketing Thinkers

### Seth Godin — The Tribe Builder
Most cited living thinker in brand-for-startups. *Purple Cow*, *Tribes*, *This Is Marketing*. Through-line: stop interrupting people and start mattering to a specific audience. His blog remains one of the best daily reads. If advising on TodoScope, he'd say: "Stop describing the scanner. Describe the feeling of knowing your codebase is honest."

### Marty Neumeier — The Brand Gap
*The Brand Gap*, *Zag*. Systems-level thinking about what a brand actually is (a gut feeling, not a logo). Especially relevant for early-stage companies defining positioning.

### Martin Lindstrom — Sensory Branding
Neuromarketing, emotional resonance. More established-brand focused, but ideas on sensory cues are widely cited.

## Rising Voices

### Ben Gilbert & David Rosenthal — Acquired Podcast
Deep-dive company histories (Nike, LVMH, Nvidia). Exceptional for understanding how iconic brands were built over decades.

### Sam Parr & Shaan Puri — My First Million
Trend-spotting, business idea generation, founder-market fit. Accessible.

### Harry Stebbings — 20VC
Best access to top-tier VCs. Useful for understanding how investors think about brand and growth.

## The 2025-2026 Through-Line

Founder-led branding is ascendant. Customers want a person behind the company, not just a logo. Godin and Horowitz both point at this from different angles. TodoScope's brand interview reflects this: honest founder voice, clarity-first, AI agents as teammates.

## How This Applies to TodoScope

- **Godin's tribe**: our tribe is small teams, solo founders, underdogs who track issues in the code
- **Horowitz's culture test**: we open-source, we self-host, we dogfood — culture is visible in the product
- **Neumeier's gut feeling**: TodoScope should feel honest, lightweight, no-lock-in on first impression
- **Founder-led**: Alexander & Izzy's voice runs through all copy (per brand interview)
