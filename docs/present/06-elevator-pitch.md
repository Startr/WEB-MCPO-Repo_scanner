# TodoScope — Elevator Pitch

## The Cocktail Party Sentence

> "Your projects already know what's unfinished. TodoScope just makes it visible — to your whole team, including AI."

Use this as:
- The one-liner in conversation
- The subtitle on a landing page or slide
- The opening of a cold email or social post
- The sentence someone repeats to a friend the next day

---

## Why This Works

1. **"Your projects already know"** — positions the user's existing work as smart, not broken. The problem isn't them — it's that nothing was surfacing what's already there.

2. **"TodoScope just makes it visible"** — "just" does heavy lifting. It implies simplicity. No configuration, no learning curve, no extra work. The tool gets out of the way.

3. **"to your whole team"** — immediately communicates collaboration, shared visibility, cognitive accessibility. Not a solo developer tool — a team tool.

4. **"including AI"** — the turn. Expands the definition of "team" without requiring the listener to understand agent protocols, MCP, or any infrastructure. Two words. Paradigm shift.

---

## Variations by Context

### For developers
> "TodoScope scans your repos and builds a Kanban board from your TODOs, FIXMEs, and BUGs — one config, zero maintenance."

### For project managers
> "TodoScope gives you a live project board that updates itself from the source. No tickets to create. No statuses to update."

### For AI/tech audiences
> "TodoScope is an MCP-compatible awareness layer — your AI agents can inspect what's unfinished across every repo, the same way your humans can."

### For investors (if asked)
> "We're building the awareness layer for AI-assisted development. TodoScope is where agents and humans see the same truth about what needs doing."

## The Extended Pitch (30 seconds)

"Every project tracking system dies because someone has to maintain it. Jira rots, Trello goes stale, spreadsheets get abandoned. Everything fades becuase of extra work; because updating the tracker is that extra work on top of the actual work.

Your projects already know what's unfinished. Every TODO comment, every FIXME, every task in your TODO.md. TodoScope reads your code and builds the board — a live Kanban that maintains itself. Everyone on your team can see what needs doing, including AI. No setup. No maintenance. No extra work.

It's open source. It's free. And your AI teammates see the same board your humans do."
