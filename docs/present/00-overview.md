# TodoScope Presentation Scaffold

## Overview

This presentation scaffold serves three delivery modes from a single narrative:

1. **10-minute keynote** — tight, live, high-impact
2. **15-minute walkthrough** — includes extended demo and guided tour
3. **Recorded / deck format** — self-paced, shareable

## Core Message

**"There's no extra work."**

TodoScope reads your repo and builds the board. You don't maintain a separate tracking system. You update your code, and the board updates itself. Everyone on your team can see what needs doing — even AI.

## Audience

- **Primary:** New users discovering TodoScope for the first time
- **Secondary:** Potential investors observing the ecosystem

| Beat | Title | Time (Keynote) | Core Idea |
