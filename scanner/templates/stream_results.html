How this Kanban board works

The board is generated from your code. Edit your TODO.md or inline comments, rescan, and the board updates.

ColumnHow items get there
Backlog## Low Priority or ## Backlog section
TODO## Medium Priority or any unlisted section · inline # TODO:
In Progress## In Progress, ## Doing, or ## High Priority · inline # FIXME:
Bugs## Bugs section · inline # BUG:
DoneAny - [x] checked item, or the ## Completed section

To move an item to In Progress: move it under a ## In Progress section in your TODO.md, or change the inline comment to # FIXME:.

Tags: Tags describe the kind of work. Section headers and inline keywords determine which column a card lands in. The tag #critical elevates a card to the top of its column. The tag #bug moves a card to the Bugs column. No other tag affects placement.
