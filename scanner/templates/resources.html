Kanban Resources

Resources for understanding Kanban — from Toyota's factory floors to modern software delivery.

Videos

Toyota's Kanban System

Where it all started. See how Toyota used physical cards to signal demand and control work-in-progress on the production line. The principles here — pull, limit WIP, make work visible — are exactly what software kanban boards inherit.

Kanban Deep-Dive Playlist

Kanban from principles to practice. Covers flow metrics, WIP limits, pull policies, and how to evolve your process incrementally.

Books

Kanban: Successful Evolutionary Change for Your Technology Business — David J. Anderson

The foundational text for applying Kanban to knowledge work. Anderson adapted Toyota's principles for software teams: visualize work, limit WIP, manage flow, make policies explicit, and improve collaboratively.

Kanban from the Inside — Mike Burrows

Goes deeper into the values and principles behind Kanban. Burrows explains the "why" that Anderson's book assumes — transparency, balance, collaboration, customer focus, flow, leadership, understanding, agreement, and respect.

Rethinking Agile: Why Agile Teams Have Nothing to Do with Business Agility — Klaus Leopold

Challenges common agile assumptions from a flow perspective. Leopold argues that optimizing individual teams misses the point — business agility comes from optimizing the end-to-end flow of work across the organization.
